Financial Class Definitions

  • OHCN: Valid or present Ontario Health Insurance Plan (OHIP) cardholders; funded by the Ontario provincial government.
  • OHCN (Coverage Unconfirmed): Patient forgotten Ontario Health Card at home; temporary grace period granted to provide card details before billing.
  • Refugee Health Plan: Refugee claimants or protected persons covered under the federal Interim Federal Health Program (IFHP).
  • UHIP: Non-resident international students or their covered dependents holding mandatory University Health Insurance Plan coverage.
  • Ontario Uninsured: Ontario residents lacking OHIP whose parent(s)/guardian(s) are Canadian citizens or Permanent Residents; self-pay pending OHIP enrollment.
  • Out of Province (OOP) Insured: Canadian residents living outside Ontario who hold a valid provincial/territorial health card billed via reciprocal agreement.
  • Out of Province (OOP) Uninsured: Canadian residents living outside Ontario without a valid provincial health card; billed directly as self-pay.
  • SK IPP Insurance CAD: International or non-resident patients with valid commercial/private health or travel insurance billed in CAD via the International Patient Portfolio.
  • SK IPP Self-Pay CAD: International or non-resident patients (e.g., visitors) without accepted coverage who pay out-of-pocket in CAD via the Business Office.
